# Competitive Landscape

## Crop-CASMA (USDA + NASA)
- What: Maps current soil moisture and crop conditions using SMAP + MODIS
- Who for: USDA analysts, researchers
- Gap: Monitoring only. No rotation planning. Not farmer-facing.

## OpenET
- What: Field-level evapotranspiration for irrigation decisions
- Who for: Irrigators in water-scarce regions
- Gap: Water only. No soil health trajectory. No rotation simulation.

## Our position
Monitoring tools answer "what is my soil doing now?"
Soil Memory answers "what has my soil been doing for 10 years, and what should I do next?"
